# Homepage guide motion

Approved locally on 2026-10-31. Publication requires the user's explicit “发布” or “上线”; continuation or approval does not authorize it.

## Outcome

- Four cards begin in a balanced two-by-two grid. Selecting any card moves it into a full-width top row; the other three form a compact row below on desktop. Closing restores the original grid continuously.
- Animate actual card position and dimensions, not scaled text. Interruptions start from the currently painted rectangle; resizing, hidden pages and reduced-motion preferences settle cleanly.
- Stats layers separate on expansion. Four genuine content metrics and their layers share the same hover/focus/selection highlight. Do not fabricate analytics.
- Articles form a real three-card deck. Previous/next, automatic rotation and horizontal swipe animate the outgoing card; hover/focus/page visibility pause auto-rotation independently.
- Index illustration reveals a short typed phrase, then branches/nodes. Category and tag selectors are mutually exclusive and animated. Contacts use staged node motion and only confirmed internal destinations.
- Settings popover opens from the trigger with scale/translation/fade and reverses on close. Closing content is immediately inert and inaccessible. Theme selection has one sliding indicator.
- Preserve native cursor, all routing, genuine content, existing keyboard focus behavior, hero, authentication and commerce functionality.
- Keep the existing warm-paper/violet identity and original artwork rather than copying reference assets or text.
